Nine zones, each with a focus

The JS-SEZ spans more than 3,500 km² with nine investment zones across 11 sectors. Each zone targets certain activities, but you are free to set up in whichever zone suits you.

The SETUP framework

The playbook's five stages for moving from interest to a running operation.

  1. S

    Select your zone strategically

    Match your activities to a zone's focus to benefit from its infrastructure and incentives.

  2. E

    Evaluate incentives and requirements

    Check MIDA's JS-SEZ tax packages and EnterpriseSG's support schemes against your plans.

  3. T

    Take the right legal steps

    Choose a structure (Sdn. Bhd., LLP or representative office), register with SSM, get licences, and register for tax, EPF, SOCSO and HRDF.

  4. U

    Utilise bilateral support

    Use Singapore's JS-SEZ Project Office, Malaysia's IMFC-J one-stop centre, and SMF or FMM for partners and market entry.

  5. P

    Prepare your talent and supply chain

    Tap Johor's engineering workforce, training support and local supply-chain partners.

Tax and money

Incentives should follow your operating model, not dictate it. Decide what happens on each side of the Causeway first, then check which schemes fit.

Incentives worth knowing

5% corporate tax
For 10 or 15 years, for qualifying manufacturing projects with capital investment (excluding land) of at least RM500 million or RM1 billion.
5% personal income tax
For eligible knowledge workers in the JS-SEZ.
Smaller projects
Can still use Malaysia's standard investment incentives, plus EDB and EnterpriseSG grants on the Singapore side.
Market Readiness Assistance
Up to 50% of eligible costs, capped at S$100,000 per company per new market.

Watch out for

  • Permanent establishment. Singapore staff who regularly supervise, negotiate or make decisions in Johor can create a Malaysian tax presence for your company.
  • Transfer pricing. Sales and services between your Singapore and Johor entities must be priced at arm's length and documented in both countries.
  • Commuting staff. Workdays spent in Johor can trigger Malaysian personal tax. Treaty relief only applies when its conditions are met, so track days and activities.
  • Bank accounts take weeks. KYC checks for foreign-owned companies can be slow, so talk to banks before incorporation is final.

Bringing people across

Johor offers a growing, multilingual technical workforce, with the Johor Talent Development Council coordinating training. For staff you send in, these are the main passes.

Employment Pass
Three categories by minimum basic salary: from RM10,000 (I), RM5,000 to RM9,999 (II) and RM3,000 to RM4,999 (III). Revised minimums took effect on 1 June 2026, so check the current figures on the ESD portal.
Professional Visit Pass
For short-term professional work such as machine installation or training, on behalf of an overseas company. Up to 12 months, not extendable.
Investor Pass
A multi-entry pass for founders, directors and senior managers on business trips. Up to 12 months, six months at a time.
